Our Legacy & Commitment

Uttar Pradesh’s Most Dependable Solar EPC Contractor

Headquartered in Vibhuti Khand, Gomti Nagar, Lucknow, GreenTech Power was founded with a singular, clear mission: to accelerate clean energy adoption across Uttar Pradesh by making rooftop solar affordable, reliable, and hassle-free.

As an officially empanelled EPC partner under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana and UP NEDA (Uttar Pradesh New and Renewable Energy Development Agency), we handle every stage of your solar journey: complimentary 3D shadow analysis, MNRE-approved Tier-1 DCR solar modules, structural engineering, seamless UPPCL Net-Metering liaison, and direct-to-bank subsidy release.
