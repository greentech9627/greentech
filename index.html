How do I claim the ₹1,08,000 subsidy under PM Surya Ghar & UP NEDA?
Under the PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana, residential consumers receive up to ₹78,000 central subsidy for systems 3kW and above. Additionally, the Government of Uttar Pradesh through UP NEDA provides a state subsidy of up to ₹30,000, bringing total financial assistance to ₹1,08,000! GreenTech Power handles the entire registration and verification on the National Portal; the subsidy is deposited directly (DBT) into your linked bank account within 30-45 days of commissioning.
How long does UPPCL / MVVNL take to install the Net Meter in Lucknow?
Once our installation is complete, we submit the work completion report and test certificates to your local LESA / MVVNL sub-station. Typically, UPPCL inspects the installation and installs the bidirectional net meter within 10 to 18 business days. Our local liaison team handles all follow-ups.
Will solar panels generate electricity during Lucknow's foggy winters?
Yes! Solar panels rely on ambient photons rather than direct heat. During cloudy or foggy winter days in Uttar Pradesh, our N-Type TOPCon bifacial modules still operate at 30% to 50% capacity by capturing diffuse daylight and reflected light from the rear side. Over the 12-month annual cycle, Lucknow enjoys over 300 days of bright sunshine.
Will installing solar panels cause roof leaks or structural damage?
Not at all. We utilize non-penetrating ballasted mounts or specialized anchor fasteners with industrial EPDM rubber gaskets and dual-layer chemical waterproofing seals. Our structures are certified to withstand wind velocities of up to 160 km/h and come with a 10-year structural integrity warranty.
Can I run 1.5-Ton Air Conditioners (ACs) on a 3kW or 5kW solar system?
Absolutely. On an On-Grid system, the solar power directly powers your home loads including 1.5-Ton 5-Star inverters ACs, refrigerators, and washing machines. Any additional power required is drawn seamlessly from the UPPCL grid, and surplus solar power is exported back to credit your bill.
