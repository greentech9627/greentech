Documents Required For UP Subsidy Claim

Our Lucknow team prepares and submits your entire dossier on the National Solar Portal and UPPCL Discom portal.

Recent UPPCL (MVVNL / LESA / PVVNL / DVVNL) electricity bill with active Consumer ID and Sanctioned Load.

Copy of property owner’s Aadhaar card matching the electricity connection name for instant portal authentication.
